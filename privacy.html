Privacy Policy

This is a personal portfolio website run by Ezenwa Alphonsus Anayo. It has no accounts, no sign-up and no forms, and it does not set cookies.

What I collect

The site itself collects nothing. If you email me, I receive your email address and whatever you write. I use it only to reply and I do not share it or add it to any list.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you request, under its own privacy statement.

Fonts

Fonts load from Google Fonts. To deliver them, Google receives your IP address and browser details, under Google's own privacy policy.

Analytics

No analytics are running on this site.

Analytics

If you accept in the banner, the site uses GoatCounter, a privacy-focused analytics service. It counts page views and records the referring page, browser, screen size and country. According to the provider it uses no cookies and does not build profiles of visitors. If you decline, nothing is loaded. Your choice is stored in your browser's local storage and you can clear it at any time.

Links to other sites

This site links to services such as GitHub. They have their own privacy practices and I am not responsible for them.

Your rights

I handle personal data in line with applicable data protection law, including the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023. You can ask me what I hold about you, or ask me to delete it, using the contact section.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will change with it.
